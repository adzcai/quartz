Markov chain

2 min read

a discrete time stochastic process
taking values in the state space
is called a Markov chain (or “has the Markov property”)
if for all times ,

  • given entire past history, only the most recent term matters
  • ie given present, past and future are conditionally independent

fully described by transition matrix (usually assume time invariant)
which can also be visualized as a graph where the nodes are possible states
