1 min read

reparameterize such that velocity along the curve is constant

Consider a path . How to measure its length?

Can partition the interval into small chunks and measure the total length

Then as the mesh grows finer this becomes a Riemann integral

ie we integrate the magnitude of the tangent / velocity vector.

by chain rule this is invariant of the specific parameterization

for Riemannian manifolds we can just measure the length of the tangent vector using the metric tensor.
