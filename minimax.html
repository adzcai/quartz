minimax

1 min read

minimax decision theory:
choose solutions that protect against worst-case scenarios;
assume the opponent behaves optimally.
we frame this as the optimization problem

where is the class of “solutions” / decisions / policys,
is the class of “problems” / opponents,
and measures the risk / cost of the decision (lower is better).
eg in statistical modelling denotes the risk of the fitting method on the data generating process .
minimax is much stronger than being admissible in model assessment

see matrix minimax, least favourable prior, generative adversarial

e.g. adversarial training in deep learning:
find things that the model is bad at (especially adversarial attacks),
and specifically insert more of that into the training data;
a form of data augmentation
