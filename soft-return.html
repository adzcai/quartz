for policy learning,
the objective function is typically the policy’s return:

Link to original

when optimizing over stochastic policys,
by principle of maximum entropy,
we should add the entropy of the trajectory distribution:

plus a constant. (assume discounted sum.)
this approach maximizes cumulative entropy across the entire trajectory distribution
so the policy will try to enter states where it is “less certain”;
this drives exploration-exploitation tradeoff.

this objective is also the elbo in the optimality variable Bayesian network;
the optimality variables are observed and the trajectory is the latent variable.

minimizing the reverse kld leads to the reckless policy instead.

implementation wise,
we can just add to the reward at each step
and be sure to include it in the gradient.
