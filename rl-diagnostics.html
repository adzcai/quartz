rl diagnostics

policy entropy divided by maximum possible entropy ( for finite action space)
should start near , fall, and flatten out
if near : policy is basically acting uniformly at random
if near : policy has collapsed;
check for policy entropy loss or other exploration incentives
oscillation: decrease learning rate

kld between collection policy and current learner policy
high indicates severe off policy experience
(recall must be nonnegative)

variance of temporal difference errors divided by variance of value targets

lots of good gpu profile advice
